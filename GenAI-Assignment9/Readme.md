## NumPy Functions Used
Some of the NumPy functions used in this assignment are:

1.np.array() – creates an array.
2.np.arange() – creates a sequence of numbers.
3.np.reshape() – changes the shape of an array.
4.np.add() and np.subtract() – perform addition and subtraction.
5.np.multiply(), np.divide(), and np.power() – perform mathematical operations.
6.np.sqrt() – calculates square roots.
7.np.exp() – calculates exponential values.
8.np.log() – calculates natural logarithms.
9.np.sum() and np.cumsum() – calculate the total and cumulative sum.
10.np.mean() and np.median() – calculate the mean and median.
11.np.var() and np.std() – calculate variance and standard deviation.
12.np.min(), np.max(), and np.percentile() – find minimum, maximum, and percentile values.
13.np.sort() – sorts array values.
14.np.where() – can be used to find positions that match a condition.
